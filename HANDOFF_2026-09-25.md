### 2.2 Hazards: gas, mask, larvae

- **Gas** (`Server.Gas`): the cloud is the authored VFX in `ServerStorage.Templates.GasCloud`
  (copied from `Workspace.TestArea["Gas vfx"]`). The host part is hidden and is the damage box.
  Emitter rates scale by face area and cap at `EventConfig.Gas.MaxEmitterRate` (600). Volume scaling
  would have produced about 50k particles per second.
- **Mask** (`GasMask` tool, `MaskOverlay` GUI): masked players take no gas damage.
- **Larvae**: `Server.Larvae` is the only place a larva body is built (red cube placeholder, tag
  `Enemy`). `Server.Larva` is the brain. `Server.Enemies` ticks every larva on one Heartbeat and caps
  them at `MaxAlive` 8.
- **Larva health bar**: `StarterGui.LarvaHealth` is a BillboardGui template authored in Studio.
  `StarterPlayerScripts.EnemyHealth` clones it over every `Enemy`-tagged model.

### 2.3 Tag-bound spawners (the architecture to copy)

```
ReplicatedStorage.Config.Tags     frozen { Enemy, Egg, UFO, Survivor }; never use a string literal
Server.TagBinder.bind(tag, make)  make() on tag-add, :Destroy() on tag-remove AND Destroying
                                  only binds instances inside Workspace, so templates stay inert
Server.Egg       PLACEMENT clones Templates.Egg and tags it; BEHAVIOUR incubates 3 s then hatches
Server.UFO       BEHAVIOUR only (sweep or drop attacks)
Server.Survivor  PLACEMENT + BEHAVIOUR, same split as Egg
```

- **Parent the clone first, add the tag second.** Otherwise the binder ignores it.
- Placement and behaviour only meet through the tag. A designer can drop a tagged model anywhere in
  Studio and it works.
- A new spawner kind is one behaviour module, one tag and one template.
- Per-model attributes override config: Egg `HatchSeconds`, `Count`. UFO `Interval`, `Count`,
  `MaxDrops`, `ActivationRadius`.

### 2.4 Event blocks

A block's kind is its `BlockMaterial` attribute. Its colour is the telegraph. All use Neon, so a
buried one glows as soon as a neighbour cracks.

| Kind | Colour | HP | On break |
|---|---|---|---|
| Event | blue `60,130,235` | 80 | `EventRoll` picks Gas, Egg or Survivor at equal weight |
| Egg | pale yellow `235,220,150` | 60 | an egg appears and hatches a larva 3 s later |
| Organ | gas green `110,200,80` | 100 | a gas cloud bursts in that cell |
| Survivor | orange `240,150,50` | 80 | a survivor appears. Hand-placed only, never seeded |

- `EventBlocks.Seed` retags 24 Event and 8 Egg blocks from one shuffle of buried Flesh blocks.
- **D7:** the drone never cuts an event block. **D9:** the gun never cuts the kaiju. Both are
  enforced in `Demolition.CanDamage`.
- **EVT-16:** only one event at a time below 30 % demolition. The sandbox turns it off with
  `RunState.IgnoreSingleEventPhase`.
- `Demolition.Adopt(part)` snaps a block authored outside the kaiju onto the grid and makes it a real
  block. The test area uses it.
- Full design: `Progress/modules/Dismantle — Event Block Design.md`.

### 2.5 Survivor (Metal Slug rule)

- Waits up to 60 s. The first player within 6 studs rescues it.
- The rescuer gets **+25 coins and +30 HP** (capped at 100). A "Thank you!" bubble shows for 1.5 s.
- Counters: `RunState.SurvivorsRescued` and the player attribute `Survivors`.
- **The heal is Ayesh's call.** The sheet and drawing pay coins only. No document says it heals.

### 2.6 UFO

Every `Interval` (8 s) with a player within 120 studs, the saucer picks one attack at random
(`EventConfig.UFO.Weights`):

- **Sweep:** a red line is laid through the nearest player. The line is the saucer's route. The
  saucer flies to the near end (at least 2 s of warning), passes along it at 40 studs/s, deals 10 to
  each player it passes over once, then flies home.
- **Drop:** one larva under the beam.
- UFO and larvae ignore players whose `BriefComplete` attribute is not `true`, so nobody is hit during
  the mission brief.
- UFO parts have `CanQuery = false`, so gun rays and cutter aim pass through to larvae below.
- **Not built:** how a UFO arrives in a real run (red block or timer), and the "beam pulls a player"
  idea from the drawing.

### 2.8 Upgrade system

Three lines, three stats, three tiers. Tier 1 is the baseline.

| Line | Stat | T1 / T2 / T3 | Cost to T2 / T3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Player | Damage | ×1 / ×1.4 / ×1.9 | 25 / 75 |
| Player | Speed (s per hit) | 0.15 / 0.12 / 0.10 | 25 / 75 |
| Player | Range | 90 / 110 / 130 | 25 / 75 |
| Drone | Count | 1 / 2 / 3 | 50 / 150 |
| Drone | Damage | ×1 / ×1.65 / ×2.7 | 25 / 75 |
| Drone | Speed | ×1 / ×1.25 / ×1.5 | 25 / 75 |
| Gun | Damage | 10 / 14 / 20 | 25 / 75 |
| Gun | Fire rate | 2 / 3 / 4 per s | 25 / 75 |
| Gun | Range | 20 / 30 / 40 | 25 / 75 |

- `Config.UpgradeConfig` holds the ladders plus `Tier`, `Value`, `Attribute`, `Ladder`.
- Tiers live on the player as attributes `Upg_<Line>_<Stat>`.
- `Server.Upgrades` is the **only writer**. Remote `BuyUpgrade(line, stat)` validates both strings,
  checks max tier and cash, charges, raises the tier and replies `(line, stat, ok, reason)`.
  Rate-limited to one call per 0.25 s per player.
- Consumers (`Cutter`, `Drone`, `Gun`, `Gun.Client`, `LaserCutter.Client`) read `UpgradeConfig.Value`
  on every hit, so a purchase applies at once.
- **Removed as duplicates:** `CutterLevel`, `DroneLevel`, `ToolConfig.Gun`,
  `ToolConfig.Cutter.Range` and `LevelMult`, `DroneConfig.LevelMult`, `StartingLevel`,
  `HoverOffset`. Anything still referencing them is stale.
- **Not built:** persistence. Coins and tiers reset on rejoin.
- Full design: `Progress/modules/Dismantle — Upgrade System v2.md`.

### 2.9 UI

- **Fusion 0.3** is at `ReplicatedStorage.Fusion` (official dphfox release).
- `ReplicatedStorage.UI.Stores` mirrors server-written attributes into Fusion Values.
  `Stores.Player(scope, player)` and `Stores.Run(scope)`. Push every connection into the scope.
- `Config.UITheme` holds all colours, fonts and tween settings. Never inline a colour.
- **HUD** (`StarterGui.HUD`, wired by `StarterPlayerScripts.HUD`):
  - Cash top-left in the arcade font, with a fading `+$N` income ticker.
  - Vertical kaiju meter on the left: **blue** fill, percent to two decimals, a "N CUT" block counter.
  - Health top-right: a pixel-art heart and four LED segments with partial fills, so every 10 HP hit
    and every heal moves the bar. The border flashes red on damage, green on heal.
- **Upgrade tree** (`StarterGui.Upgrades`, wired by `StarterPlayerScripts.UpgradesUI`):
  - Flat-top hex honeycomb, 31 hexes built from rotated rectangles, no image assets.
  - Opens on the KAIJU hub with Player, Drone and Gun. Clicking a line shows its branch. The hub
    then reads ALL and returns to the root view.
  - States: owned, available, too expensive, locked silhouette. Always text plus colour.
  - Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom at the pointer. A press moving more than 6 px never buys.
  - Touch: first tap previews, second tap buys. Tooltip with a hint line.
- **Rail:** UPGRADE (U) with an affordable-count badge, VIEW (V) toggling first and third person
  (`StarterPlayerScripts.ViewToggle`).
- Font: `Enum.Font.Arcade` for display text, Montserrat Bold for body. Arcade deviates from the
  studio's Montserrat rule on purpose, to match the reference.
- Uploaded health-bar image art was tried and **reverted** at Ayesh's request. The image ids are on
  the Studio account `mohaimin11234` if the art returns.
